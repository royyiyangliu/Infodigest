Circana data on the U.S. market. Mobile numbers provided by Sensor Tower.
Market
In October 2026, Americans spent $4.245 billion on games (-12% YoY). In October 2025, it was $4.818 billion.
Americans spent $3.705 billion on content (-14% YoY).
❗️I’ll note again that part of mobile revenue is missed because of D2C’s growing popularity. The real picture for content is likely better than these numbers show.

Console game spending fell 18% YoY. Circana notes that despite Call of Duty launching in October instead of November, sales were still below last year’s. Last October happened the releases of Battlefield 6 and Pokémon Legends: Z-A.
Battlefield 6
became the best-selling game of 2025 in the U.S. right after its launch
.
The U.S. market has declined for five months in a row. In September, the drop was very small (-1%), but in October it accelerated again.
Over the first ten months of 2026, Americans spent $45.283 billion on games (-4% YoY). The gap with 2025 widened: after September,
it was 3%
.
Over ten months, Americans spent $40.044 billion on game content (-4% YoY). In September, the gap was half of that, -2%.
Hardware and Accessories
In October, Americans spent $357 million on gaming hardware (+2% YoY). Over ten months, they spent $3.261 billion (8% less than in the same period of 2025). The gap with last year narrowed slightly: in September, it was 9%.
PlayStation 5 dollar sales grew 20% YoY, and Xbox Series 42%. This offset an 11% drop in Nintendo Switch 2 sales.
That said, higher prices drove the dollar sales growth for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series. PlayStation 5 unit sales grew 5% YoY, while its average price rose 13%. Xbox Series unit sales grew 10%, and its average price rose 29%.
In September, Xbox
hit a new low in console unit sales
: the worst September on record. In October, the console’s unit sales returned to growth.
Nintendo Switch 2 led in both units and dollars in October and over the first ten months of 2026. PlayStation 5 ranked second by both metrics.
NEX Playground, a motion-controlled family console, ranked fourth in hardware unit sales in October.
Accessories spending grew 3% YoY in October to $183 million. Over ten months, Americans spent $1.977 billion on accessories (-2% YoY).
Game Sales
❗️Circana uses a methodology that combines projected estimates with actual sales. Mobile games and DLC are not included in the charts.
Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 was the best-selling game of October in the U.S. This is the 19th year in a row that a new Call of Duty release has been the best-selling game of its launch month.
The streak began in November 2008, when Call of Duty: World at War outsold Guitar Hero: World Tour and Gears of War 2.
❗️GameDiscoverCo
estimates
that Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 sold 5.35 million copies on PlayStation, 2.73 million on Xbox, and 1.74 million on Steam worldwide in October.
Gears of War: E-Day took second place, and Star Wars: Galactic Racer third. Six new releases made October’s top 10.
Madden NFL 27 climbed from 10th to seventh.
Just outside the top 10, Castlevania: Belmont’s Curse debuted at 12th, and Hellraiser: Revival at 15th.
Dragon’s Dogma II climbed from 204th to 17th thanks to the Dark Arisen expansion. Resident Evil 4 rose from 64th to 31st thanks to Halloween discounts.
Marvel’s Wolverine dropped from first place in September to 14th in October.
In the first ten months of 2026, NBA 2K27, Resident Evil: Requiem, and 007 First Light led the charts. The top eight positions didn’t change.
Madden NFL 27 climbed from 12th to ninth and entered the year-to-date top 10. In September, the game wasn’t in the ranking because Circana doesn’t combine sales of the game itself and its two bundles.
Less than $10 million separates eighth and 13th place in the year-to-date ranking.
Places 11 through 15 went to Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4, EA Sports College Football 27, EA Sports FC 27, Marvel’s Wolverine, and Pokémon Pokopia. So after just one month of sales, Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 is already close to the top 10.
