GTA VI became THE LARGEST entertainment launch ever.
Take-Two says GTA VI made $3.79B in day-one retail sales
, a new record for entertainment across any medium. The game sold 32.5M units before release and opened with 99 on Metacritic. For context, GTA V made over $800M on its first day in 2013, and GTA IV made around $310M in 2008.
Newzoo looked at who pre-ordered the game
: 52% had spent money on GTA V or GTA Online since 2018, the typical buyer spent $129 on three other premium games over the previous 12 months, and women made up less than 15% of pre-orders (vs. 27% of all premium game buyers). At the same time, 16% hadn't bought a full-price game in a year, so Newzoo expects GTA VI to “grow the market rather than simply redistribute it.” Publishers weren't so sure and cleared the way: only two AAA games came out between October 8 and November 18, compared with ten in 2025.
