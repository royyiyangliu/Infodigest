PC/Console games
According to Sensor Tower, GTA VI pre-orders have brought in over $1.4 billion
. 87% of orders were for the $100 Ultimate Edition, and 75% of buyers purchased the game on PlayStation. The game launches tomorrow!
The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake sold over 5.2 million copies in four days after release on Nintendo Switch 2
. That's the best launch among Nintendo games on Switch 2. The 1998 original sold 7.6 million copies in two years.
Borderlands franchise sales by Gearbox and 2K have surpassed 100 million copies
. The latest entry, Borderlands 4, came out just over a year ago.

Marvel's Wolverine by Insomniac Games sold 2.4 million copies in seven weeks after release
.
The Steam version of Escape from Tarkov by Battlestate Games sold over 2 million copies in a year after leaving beta
. The studio didn't disclose sales through its own website.
Mewgenics by Edmund McMillen sold over 2 million copies
. The game launched on PC in February 2026 and on consoles in September. McMillen described console sales as decent but not outstanding.
MARVEL Cosmic Invasion by Tribute Games has passed 2 million players
. The figure includes Game Pass players. The game came out in December 2025 and passed 1.5 million players in May 2026.
DREADMOOR by Dream Dock and Digital Vortex Entertainment earned over $1 million at its Early Access launch
. It's an adventure about fishing in a flooded post-apocalyptic world. By release, the game had gathered over 900 thousand wishlists.
Mobile games
Hustle Castle by MY.GAMES has earned $525 million in nine years
. The game has been downloaded over 87 million times.
According to AppMagic, Monster Hunter Outlanders by TiMi Studio Group earned over $10.2 million in in-app purchases in its first two weeks after release
. The game was downloaded 5.1 million times.
Minecraft's annual audience in China has reached 100 million players
. NetEase has published the game in the country since 2017. The platform has over 400 thousand content creators, and their earnings grew 30% year over year.
Platforms
PlayStation 5 shipments have surpassed 100 million consoles
. It took almost six years, while PS4 reached the same milestone in five years and seven months.
