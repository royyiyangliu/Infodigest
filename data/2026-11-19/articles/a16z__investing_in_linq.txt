2026 is the year AI agents crossed into the mainstream, accounting for
more than half
of internet traffic. They are taking on a growing role in both our personal and professional lives: planning trips, booking restaurant reservations, scheduling business meetings, and completing tasks delegated to them much like a coworker could. We believe that AI agents will become the primary way we interact with the companies and services we love, and companies will need to adapt to that new reality and enable their customers to communicate with them through agents.
At the same time, agents have been the most useful when they fit into the way we already live and work, introducing novel experiences through familiar modes of communication. Using AI agents today feels natural, almost like asking another person for help.
That’s thanks in large part to
Linq
, which has emerged as a leading infrastructure platform for this shift. Linq gives developers the tools to build reliable, scalable messaging experiences while handling the operational complexity behind them. Many of the fastest-growing agent companies today use Linq to connect their agents directly to customers. Large enterprises like DoorDash have similarly met the moment with Linq: instead of purely interacting with users in-app or through notifications, DoorDash is working with Linq to allow its consumers to find food, build a cart, and check out in a single thread on Apple Messages for Business. As an official Apple Messages for Business MSP, Linq can help more enterprises launch and scale customer-facing agents on the same channel. We believe every business in the future should be on Linq to meet their customers where they already are.
Linq’s customers rave about the product and team. They say that Linq has felt like an extension of their dev teams and find the product to be delightfully easy to integrate. In addition, they are an approved Messaging Service Provider with Apple, which gives customers confidence that they can legitimately build their businesses on Linq. The company also offers RCS, WhatsApp and other platforms today, with the ambition to become the platform that helps all businesses and agents communicate wherever their users are.
Our conviction in the founders has been years in the making, making this announcement particularly rewarding. We first met founder/CEO Elliott Potter all the way back in 2020, on Zoom in the midst of COVID, when Linq was building digital business cards in Birmingham, Alabama. We loved Elliott’s enthusiasm for business building even back then, and even took a screenshot to memorialize that first meeting!
Since then, we’ve watched them evolve Linq from digital business cards to software for sales teams, then AI sales agents, and ultimately communications infrastructure for AI agents. This final transition came when customers came to Linq organically, asking to use the messaging technology underneath the application for their own purposes. The founders heard the feedback, recognized the opportunity in the infrastructure they’d built for their own AI agent, and rebuilt the company around it. Now they are in an ideal position to meet this moment.
Their journey is one of the best stories of grit, persistence, and true founder energy we’ve seen. Elliott, Patrick and Jared built their business in Alabama for the first 6 years, far from the eye of Silicon Valley. As a result, they were consistently underestimated for years, and Elliott has
written publicly
about nearly running out of money multiple times. Once the business required it, they uprooted their lives and families to move to San Francisco to be closer to their customers. Our whole team has enormous admiration for their resilience: the willingness to reinvent the business multiple times, the determination to find product-market fit, and the sheer number of years they put into getting here. They’re true entrepreneurs.
Having watched Linq evolve since 2020, we couldn’t be more excited to finally partner with Elliott, Patrick, Jared, and the entire team. We are thrilled to be leading Linq’s Series B. If you’re excited by the mission and the team, they’re hiring!
