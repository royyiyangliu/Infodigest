Unity started making money
Unity posted its first net income since its IPO, as its Vector ad network grew 126% YoY
. Net income was $122M (vs. a $127M loss a year ago), and revenue grew 32% to $622M. That’s been a long road, and it seems that Unity is doing well under the new management.
Almost three-quarters of revenue ($457M, +44%) now comes from ads, while the engine business grew 8% to $165M. So, basically, Unity is increasingly an ad company that also makes an engine.
I think that's a good sign for the mobile market. Unity expects its strategic ads revenue to double YoY next quarter, and it has just partnered with PvX to finance UA for mobile studios. Whatever people say about mobile, money keeps flowing into UA.
