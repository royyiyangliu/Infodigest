PC/Console games
The Call of Duty franchise's lifetime revenue has surpassed $40 billion
. Xbox CEO Asha Sharma shared the figure following the launch of Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4. In April 2026, Activision cited $35 billion.
Phantom Blade Zero by S-GAME sold over 4 million copies in 72 hours after release
. Its Steam peak reached almost 834 thousand concurrent players, the 14th-highest result in the platform's history.
Probably Stolen by Questing Goose Studio, and tinyBuild sold 500 thousand copies in two days after release
. Its Steam peak exceeded 152 thousand concurrent players, a record for tinyBuild.

According to The Verge, Gears of War: E-Day by The Coalition sold over 400 thousand copies in three weeks after release
. The figure doesn't include Game Pass players, who got the game on day one.
Dispatch by AdHoc Studio has passed 5 million players in a year after release
. The game is coming to iOS and Android by the end of the year as a free-to-play title.
Zombie survival RTS Ebbtide by solo developer ProjectAlive gathered 200 thousand wishlists in a week after its reveal
. The first 100 thousand came in three days. The developer uses generative AI for part of the music, voice, illustrations, 3D models, and translation.
Mobile games
Homescapes by Playrix has been downloaded over 773 million times in nine years
.
Events
gamescom asia x Thailand Game Show 2026 in Bangkok attracted 219.5 thousand visitors from 113 countries
. The business area drew 6.9 thousand visitors, up 24% from 2025.
