Title   : Announcing the Third a16z New Media Fellowship
Date    : 2026-10-26 16:58 UTC
Link    : https://www.a16z.news/p/announcing-the-third-a16z-nmf
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

More than 130 people across two cohorts have now joined the a16z New Media Fellowship, an 8-week cohort of world-class creators, writers, marketers, podcasters, and behind-the-scenes operators.
Over the past year, the fellows have been busy. They’ve founded and raised money for new companies like
Energy
,
Osmo
, and
Underdog
. They’ve led launches at fast-growing startups like Thatch, Ghost, and Exa, and built new media properties like
MTS
,
Substrate Magazine
, and
SOTA Wives
. Others have taken on new roles at companies like
OpenAI
and
Prime Intellect
, and a few
even
joined
our own New Media team. You can read about many more of them
here
.
Two cohorts in, we’ve learned that the best parts were the simplest: namely, good dinners and a vibrant, terminally online group chat.
So
we’re launching the New Media Fellowship Cohort #3
and doubling down on exactly that.
NMF3 will run for eight weeks, starting in January 2027. Dinners will be hosted in both San Francisco and New York – but fellows can be based anywhere, and everyone will be in the group chat.
