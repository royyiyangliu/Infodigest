Today, we’re introducing the
a16z Ops Engineering Fellowship
, a six-week cohort for the innovators redefining how organizations operate with AI.
AI is no longer just an individual productivity tool. The fastest growing companies in tech are now deploying it at the systems level: rolling out agents to handle cross-team tasks, redesigning workflows to eliminate manual handoffs, and building durable infrastructure that enables a company to up-level its capabilities.
The people leading this shift are cross-functional leaders who sit at the intersection of operations and engineering. They might be AI-native operations leaders, scrappy engineers, or technical founders who are transforming the infrastructure of their organization and operational stack. They identify how to leverage AI internally, then build and implement the systems that empower each team member.
We’re launching this fellowship to bring these builders together to share what’s working, level up, and define the frontier of internal AI playbooks.

The rising need for AI Operations
Every company is asking the same question:
How do we leverage AI to transform how our team operates?
Granting teams access to AI tools is only the beginning. The fastest-growing companies in tech are turning this patchwork of siloed team-wide productivity gains into one shared system. The leaders of Internal AI understand existing operational systems, see how AI drives productivity team by team, and translate those gains into infrastructure that scales across the company.
Operations is now an engineering problem, and this group is solving it.
We’re looking for fellows who
Built or deployed AI agents and systems that redesign workflows across functions (operations, product, go-to-market, etc.)
Turned AI from an individual productivity tool into durable organizational infrastructure
Think in systems rather than isolated features or one-off automations
Can navigate both the technical and organizational realities of deployment
Care deeply about measurable outcomes, internal adoption, and the craft of building better companies
You might call yourself an Internal AI Lead, Chief of Staff, Internal Tools Engineer, Platform Lead, or something else entirely. Titles matter less than what you’ve built, the work speaks for itself.
