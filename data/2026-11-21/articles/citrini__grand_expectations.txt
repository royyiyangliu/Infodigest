Introduction
We’ve finally reached the long-awaited launch of Grand Theft Auto VI!
After several years of delays, a multi-billion dollar price tag, and countless iterations of the “we got X before GTA VI” meme –
Rockstar Games (owned by Take-Two, TTWO US)
has finally released what is arguably the most anticipated video game in history.
@hi.im.vijay
– Threads
By every measure, it appears to have lived up to the hype.
The review embargo was lifted on Wednesday, and the reception has been overwhelmingly positive. GTA VI debuted with a 99 on Metacritic – tying
The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time
for the highest score in the platform’s history.
And rightfully so – Grand Theft Auto VI was intended to be a more ambitious, bigger expansion of its predecessor. Rockstar spent the last decade building a virtual replica of South Florida, including the cities of Miami and Miami Beach, the Keys, and of course, the inevitable cameos of
Florida Man.
Rockstar hasn’t publicly disclosed the price tag associated with GTA VI, though estimates point to development spending greater than $1 billion, a considerable step-up from the ~$140 million it took to create GTA V. After accounting for marketing and advertising expenses, the total investment could plausibly approach $1.5-$2 billion.
If you’re reading this in Miami, you probably know what we’re talking about. In the past few days, you might’ve seen a billboard or two for a sports team you didn’t know existed.
Fear not, there is no Mandela Effect at play here.
