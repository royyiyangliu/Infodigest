Title   : Welcome Edgard Kagan

Across the Indo-Pacific, countries are making decisions about the technologies that will underpin their economies and security for decades to come. For American technology companies, helping meet those needs requires understanding the region’s distinct markets, government priorities, and global relationships.
That is central to our Global Affairs mission: helping our founders build partnerships that advance the adoption of American technology and strengthen America and our allies.
Today, I’m pleased to welcome Ambassador Edgard Kagan as an Advisor to a16z Global Affairs.
Edgard brings decades of diplomatic experience across the Indo-Pacific. He served as U.S. Ambassador to Malaysia under Presidents Biden and Trump, following his service as Special Assistant to the President and Senior Director for East Asia and Oceania at the National Security Council. He currently holds the Freeman Chair in China Studies and serves as a Senior Adviser at the Center for Strategic and International Studies.
His experience spans the region: leading diplomatic missions as chargé d’affaires and deputy chief of mission in New Delhi, serving as deputy chief of mission in Kuala Lumpur and consul general in Mumbai, and working at the U.S. Embassies in Beijing and Canberra. In Washington, he also served as Deputy Assistant Secretary for Australia, New Zealand, and the Pacific as well as Director of Korean Affairs.
That combination of regional knowledge and experience in government matters for our founders. A company building AI infrastructure, cybersecurity tools, or defense capabilities needs to understand how its technology fits a country’s needs, how decisions are made, and which partnerships can support lasting adoption. Those answers differ across countries, and understanding the differences is essential to building trust and making progress.
Edgard will advise Global Affairs and our portfolio companies as we work through these questions, bringing his perspective on regional developments, government priorities, and opportunities for collaboration. His experience will help founders make informed decisions and build relationships with partners across the Indo-Pacific.
Our goal is to turn shared interests into practical capabilities: more secure infrastructure, stronger defenses, and technologies that help countries grow and provide for their citizens. Edgard will make that work stronger.
Please join me in welcoming him to Andreessen Horowitz.
