Title   : Meet the a16z Ops Engineering Fellows

We’re excited to announce the fellows who will be part of the inaugural a16z Ops Engineering Fellowship, an 8-week cohort of leaders who are transforming their organizations with AI.
We received thousands of applications from talented people around the world. Today, we’re thrilled to share more about the inaugural cohort of 50 fellows who are leading operations engineering at companies like Ramp, Decagon, Luma AI, Opendoor, and ElevenLabs.
See a full list of the fellows here!
Many sit at the intersection of operations and engineering, scaling operational rigor within and across teams.  They’re leading the charge by building the internal tools that consolidate the tooling stack, designing the data and context layers that agents draw on, deploying agentic workflows with human checkpoints, and driving adoption.
