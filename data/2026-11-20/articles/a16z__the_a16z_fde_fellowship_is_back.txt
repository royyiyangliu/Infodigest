Today we’re
announcing the second cohort of the
a16z FDE Fellowship
.
Our
first cohort
brought together leaders from OpenAI, ElevenLabs, HappyRobot, and other category definers. Many helped develop the original FDE model at Palantir and are now adapting it to companies with very different products, customers, and ambitions.
As AI models get more capable, customers increasingly expect software that fits the contours of their business. They want it to understand their processes, work with their data, and follow through on the job they hired it to do. The promise is becoming much bigger, and so is the work of making good on it.
The fellowship is an 8-week cohort for FDEs and Applied AI leaders, kicking off in January 2027. We’ll host dinners and events every other week, mostly in SF and NYC, but fellows can be based anywhere.
Applications are
open now
.
